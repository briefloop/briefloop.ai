Traceable delivery

Every important claim can be questioned.

Delivery is not “the model says it is finished” — it means pinning each claim back to the line of source text, the PDF page or the cell it came from.

  • Claim binding Important claims are pinned to a line of source text, PDF page, Excel cell or image — never a bare “as far as I know”. Once the body text changes, the affected bindings are re-checked.
  • Read-only review The Reviewer checks fixed versions of artifacts and sources. Read-only isolation depends on runtime support; a prompt alone does not establish isolation.
  • Audit bundle Export a zip that carries originals, excerpts or pointers only, chosen per source; it verifies offline against structure and consistency, and holds no credentials or hidden reasoning.
  • Version binding The final Word file is bound to its independent review and evidence version; earlier finals are kept, and a correction or update becomes a new version.

Before you start

Do I need a model subscription?

Actual generation needs an authenticated runtime and an available model. Model and search providers charge for their services; BriefLoop does not include their credits.

Read the guide
Will my materials leave this computer?

Workspaces and records are local. Cloud-model requests may include relevant sources, drafts and feedback. Search and connectors also send requests to their configured services.

Read the guide
Does a high score mean the report is ready to deliver?

A score is one signal for a specific version. Inspect evidence, scope and unresolved findings. A downloadable working draft is not automatically a formally verified deliverable.

Read the guide
